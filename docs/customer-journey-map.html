Stage by stage

What happens at each stage

01

Trigger: a document arrives

Mood

"The owner sent the agreement on WhatsApp and wants it signed by Friday. It's eight pages of English."

What the user does

  • Gets the document by paper, email or chat.
  • Tries to read it and stops after page two.
  • Asks a friend or relative, who is also unsure.

Pain points

  • Legal and financial language is hard to follow.
  • Important terms hide in long paragraphs.
  • A lawyer or CA costs money and time.

How Doc Decode helps

  • Made for the everyday documents people actually receive.
  • Works in English and Malayalam.
02

Discover: opening Doc Decode

Mood

"It says it runs on my own computer. Let me try the sample rental agreement first."

What the user does

  • Lands on the Home page.
  • Reads the privacy note.
  • Clicks "Explore Demo" on a sample document.
  • Sees the health badge showing whether the local AI is ready.

Pain points

  • Distrust of uploading private papers.
  • Doesn't know what the tool can or cannot do.

How Doc Decode helps

  • No cloud, no account, no database.
  • Demo documents (rental, loan, hospital bill, government notice) show real results.
  • Health badge is honest about AI status.
03

Upload: giving it the document

Mood

"I'll just take a photo of the paper copy. Hope it reads it."

What the user does

  • Drags and drops a file, or picks one.
  • Chooses the language for explanations.

Pain points

  • Not sure which file types work.
  • Large or odd files get rejected without a reason in other tools.

How Doc Decode helps

  • Accepts PDF, JPG and PNG up to 15 MB and 40 pages.
  • If a file is rejected, the message says why and what to try.
04

Wait: the analysis runs

Mood

"It's on 'Asking the local AI model'. Twelve seconds so far."

What the user does

  • Watches four steps tick off: Checking the file, Reading the text, Finding facts and clauses, Asking the local AI model.

Pain points

  • Scanned pages and Malayalam explanations take longer.
  • Waiting with no feedback feels broken.

How Doc Decode helps

  • Step list and seconds counter show it is working.
  • Rules-only results arrive in about 2 seconds.
  • If the AI is off, the AI step is simply not shown.
05

First look: the Overview

Mood

"Rental agreement. Rent ₹18,000, deposit ₹50,000, 11-month lock-in. Two things flagged red."

What the user does

  • Sees the document type and how sure the app is.
  • Reads the plain-language summary.
  • Looks at the top flagged clauses first.

Pain points

  • Fear that an AI summary is guessing.
  • Too many warnings would feel like noise.

How Doc Decode helps

  • If unsure of the type, it says "uncertain" instead of guessing.
  • Clauses are ranked so the most important come first.
  • Each explanation says whether it came from the AI or the rules.
06

Dig deeper: checking the details

Mood

"The late fee is 2% per day. That's on page 5, here's the exact sentence."

What the user does

  • Extracted data: amounts, dates, rates, line items.
  • Verify: check totals add up; compare an invoice with a purchase order.
  • Findings & actions: risky clauses with what to do.
  • Deadlines: countdown to each date.
  • Tender checklist: requirements to tick against own papers.

Pain points

  • Hard to know whether to trust each number.
  • Comparing two documents by hand is slow.

How Doc Decode helps

  • Every value links to its sentence and page.
  • Missing values say "Not found", never a guess.
  • Never says "you are eligible"; it lists requirements to check.
07

Ask: getting answers to own questions

Mood

"Can the owner keep my deposit if I leave after six months?"

What the user does

  • Types a question in "Ask Doc Decode".
  • Reads the answer and the evidence sentences.
  • Switches to Malayalam and plays it aloud for a parent.

Pain points

  • Generic chatbots answer from outside the document.
  • Reading is tiring for some family members.

How Doc Decode helps

  • Answers only from this document, with proof shown.
  • Read aloud uses offline voices, including Malayalam via eSpeak NG.
08

Act: deciding and following through

Mood

"I asked the owner to change the lock-in to 6 months. The notice date is in my calendar."

What the user does

  • Ticks the deadlines that matter.
  • Exports them as one calendar file.
  • Signs, pays, replies to the notice, or asks for changes.
  • Comes back for the next document.

Pain points

  • Dates get forgotten after the document is filed away.

How Doc Decode helps

  • Only dates the user chose go to the calendar.
  • Dates that couldn't be confirmed are never shown as upcoming or overdue.
Make or break

Moments that decide trust

Stage 2

"Is my data safe?"

If users doubt privacy, they never upload. Local-only processing and demo documents answer this before they commit.

Stage 5

"Are these numbers real?"

One invented figure destroys trust. Rules pick every number, and each one shows its source line.

Stage 8

"Did it help me act?"

The journey succeeds only if the user leaves with a decision and the dates saved.
