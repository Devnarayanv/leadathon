Step by step

The main path

1

Open Doc Decode

You

Screen: Home page at localhost:5173

You

  • Open the app in the browser.
  • Choose English or Malayalam at the top.
  • Optional: click Explore Demo to try a sample document.

App

  • Checks if the local AI is running.
  • Shows a health badge: ready, or rules only.
  • Lists the demo documents.
2

Upload the document

You

Screen: Upload area on the Home page

You

  • Drag and drop a file, or click to choose one.
  • Allowed: PDF, JPG, PNG.

App

  • Sends the file to the local backend.
  • Shows the "Working on your document" card.
3

Check the file

App

Status shown: "Checking the file"

Is the file safe and within limits? (type, real content, max 15 MB, max 40 pages)
YesContinue to step 4.
NoShow a clear message, for example "Please upload a PDF, JPG or PNG under 15 MB". Back to step 2.
4

Read the text

App

Status shown: "Reading the text"

Does the page have real text, or is it a scan or photo?
Has textRead it directly. Fast.
Scan or photoRun OCR (English + Malayalam) on that page only. Slower.

If no text can be read at all, the app says the document is unreadable and asks for a clearer copy.

5

Find what matters

App

Status shown: "Finding facts and clauses". All done by fixed rules, so the same document always gives the same result.

Works out

  • Document type from 12 types, or "uncertain".
  • Key facts: rent, deposit, loan, interest, EMI, totals.
  • Deadlines: due dates and notice periods.

Also flags

  • Risky clauses in 12 categories: auto-renewal, penalties, late fees, lock-in, one-sided terms and more.
  • Each item keeps its sentence and page number.
6

Explain in plain words

Local AI

Status shown: "Asking the local AI model"

Is the local AI running?
YesSend the top flagged clauses (up to 6, or 3 in Malayalam) in one request. Get simple rewrites back.
NoSkip this step. Everything else still shows, with a note that plain-language explanations are missing.

The AI only rewrites. It never picks what is important and never gives a number.

7

Review the results

You

Screen: Analysis workspace with tabs (details in the next section)

You

  • Start on Overview: type, summary, top risks.
  • Open other tabs as needed.
  • Check each fact against its source sentence and page.

If the type is wrong

  • Use Change category to pick the right one.
  • The app re-runs the rules on the same text. No re-upload.
8

Ask questions or listen

YouLocal AI

Screen: Ask Doc Decode tab, Read aloud button

You

  • Type a question, for example "Can the deposit be kept if I leave early?"
  • Press Read aloud to hear an explanation.

App

  • Finds the most relevant sentences in the document.
  • The AI answers from those only and shows them as evidence.
  • Speaks using the browser voice, or the offline Malayalam voice.

Repeat steps 7 and 8 until every doubt is cleared.

9

Save deadlines and decide

You

Screen: Deadlines tab

You

  • Tick the dates you care about.
  • Click export to get one calendar file (.ics).
  • Open it in Google, Outlook or phone calendar.
  • Then sign, pay, reply or negotiate.

App

  • Builds the calendar file from ticked dates only.
  • Keeps nothing afterwards. The document is gone when the page is closed.
Inside step 7

What each tab is for

One document, several views. A tab with a number shows how many items it found.

Overview

Everyone · start here
  1. See the document type and how sure the app is.
  2. Read the plain-language summary.
  3. Check the top flagged risks.

You get: the main picture in under a minute.

Extracted data

Bills, loans, invoices
  1. Scan the list of amounts, rates and dates.
  2. View line items where present.
  3. See the sentence and page each value came from.

You get: every number, with proof.

Verify

Invoices, bills
  1. See whether totals and line items add up.
  2. Upload a second document, such as a purchase order, to compare.
  3. Review the matches and mismatches.

You get: billing errors caught before paying.

Deadlines

Everyone
  1. See each date with a countdown.
  2. Dates the app couldn't confirm are kept apart.
  3. Tick the ones to save and export.

You get: no missed due dates.

Findings & actions

Agreements, notices
  1. Read each flagged clause in original wording.
  2. Read its simple explanation and what to do.
  3. Note whether it came from the AI or the rules.

You get: what to question before signing.

Tender checklist

Tender notices
  1. See every requirement the tender asks for.
  2. Enter your business details.
  3. Tick each item against your own papers.

You get: a checklist. The app never decides eligibility.

Ask Doc Decode

Everyone
  1. Type a question in your own words.
  2. Read the answer and the evidence sentences.
  3. Ask a follow-up.

You get: answers taken only from this document.

When things don't go to plan

What if…

SituationWhat the user seesWhat to do
Wrong file type (e.g. .docx)Upload rejected with the reasonSave as PDF or take a photo, then upload again
File over 15 MB or 40 pagesUpload rejected with the limitUpload only the pages that matter
Scanned or blurry photoSlower reading, some facts "Not found"Retake the photo in good light, flat and straight
Handwritten noteLittle or wrong textNot supported reliably. Use a typed copy
Local AI not runningHealth badge shows rules only; no plain-language rewriteResults still usable. Start Ollama to get explanations
Document type unclear"Document type uncertain"Pick the right type with Change category
No Malayalam voice installedRead aloud is disabled for Malayalam, with a noteInstall eSpeak NG, or read the text
A fact isn't in the document"Not found", never a guessCheck the original or ask in the Ask tab
