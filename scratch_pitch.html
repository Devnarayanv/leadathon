  4. Malayalam-speaking users in Kerala who need documents decoded in their own language
  5. Anyone who has ever signed something they didn't fully understand — that's basically everyone

⚡ What Does It Do? (Demo Flow — 2 min)

  1. 1Upload a PDF, photo, or scan → drag & drop
  2. 2Auto-classifies the document (rental, loan, hospital bill, government notice, invoice, tender)
  3. 3Extracts hard facts: rent amount, deposit, interest rate, EMI, due dates, totals — with page numbers
  4. 4Flags risky clauses across 12 categories with attention levels (⚠️ attention → 🔴 unusual)
  5. 5Explains each clause in plain language — "This means if you leave before 6 months, you lose your entire deposit"
  6. 6Deadlines dashboard → tick the ones that matter → export to Google Calendar
  7. 7Ask questions — "What happens if I pay late?" → grounded answer with evidence
  8. 8Read aloud — works in Malayalam too (offline voice)
  9. 9Compare documents — match invoice vs. purchase order, spot mismatches
  10. 10Tender eligibility checker — check if you qualify for a government tender

🔥 What Makes It Attractive / Unique?

1. AI That Can't Hallucinate Facts

Facts come from regex + rules → deterministic, reproducible, never invented. The LLM only explains what the rules already found. If Ollama is down, the app still works.

2. 100% Offline & Private

No cloud. No API key. No data stored. No database. Document is in memory for one request, then dropped. Perfect for sensitive documents.

3. Bilingual — English + Malayalam

Full Malayalam OCR, fact extraction, and UI. Handles 18,500 രൂപ, 11 മാസം. Chillu character folding for OCR accuracy.

4. Works on Scans & Photos

OCR fallback with Tesseract — only for pages that need it. Upload a photo from WhatsApp → it works.

  • Calendar export for deadlines — "Never miss a reply-by date again"
  • 118 tests, including false-positive tests and prompt injection protection
